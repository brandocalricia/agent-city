Agent City Day 1

How to explore

  • Orbit: drag to rotate, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom
  • Click a building, label, or agent to open its panel
  • 1–4 fly to a landmark, 0 overview
  • Tab walk mode: WASD move, mouse look, Shift run, E open what you're looking at
  • Q toggle quality (laptop saver), Esc close panel
